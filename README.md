# 💎 My Atlas of Gems & Crystals (+ NatGeo Rock Tumbler Lab)

An interactive iPad & desktop educational web app inspired by **Trains Atlas**, designed to teach kids about world gemstones, crystals, the **Mohs Hardness Scale (1–10)**, and how to use their **National Geographic Gem Dig Kit & Rock Tumbler**!

👉 **Live App (GitHub Pages)**: [https://melvinjosej.github.io/gems-atlas/](https://melvinjosej.github.io/gems-atlas/)

## ✨ Features
- **🌍 Interactive World Gem Map (65 Countries)**: Tap 65 gem-producing countries across every continent with smooth auto-zoom, pan, region quick-jump buttons, and map highlight filters (`🌍 All`, `⛏️ NatGeo Kit`, `🌀 Tumbler Best`).
- **⛏️ NatGeo Gem Kit & Rock Tumbler Integration**: Every stone from the National Geographic Gem Dig Kit, Geode Kit, and Rock Tumbler Starter/Refill packs is tagged with `⛏️ In NatGeo Kit!` and includes a dedicated **Rock Tumbler Guide** tab explaining whether it's safe to tumble (`🌀 Great to Tumble!`, `⚠️ Gentle Tumble`, or `🛑 Do Not Tumble`).
- **🌀 Interactive Rock Tumbler & Mohs Scale Lab**:
  1. **Virtual 4-Stage Tumbler Simulator**: Step through Stage 1 (Coarse Grit #1), Stage 2 (Medium Grit #2), Stage 3 (Pre-Polish #3), and Stage 4 (Final Aluminum Oxide Polish #4) with animated tumbling barrel & sound effects!
  2. **"Can I Tumble These Together?" Batch Checker**: Tap any combination of gems to check if their Mohs hardness matches or if softer/fragile stones (like Pyrite, Fluorite, Opal, or Desert Rose) would get ruined.
  3. **Interactive Mohs Hardness Ladder (1–10)**: Tap any hardness level from Talc (1) to Quartz (7 — The Tumbler Sweet Spot!) to Diamond (10) to hear everyday scratch-test comparisons read aloud.
- **🔊 Kid-Friendly Voice Narrator & Microphone Search**: Auto-reads gem facts and tumbler tips aloud (with a dedicated `🔊 Read` button), and lets kids tap the microphone or press **Spacebar** to say any gem name (e.g. *"Amethyst"*, *"Tiger's Eye"*, *"Fool's Gold"*, *"Diamond"*) or country! Includes a `🔧` Hardware Microphone Selector & Test Recorder.
- **🧰 My Gem Passport & Real-Life Collector Box**: Collect country stamps as you explore the map and check off **`✅ I Have It!`** for gems in your real-life collection.
- **🎮 Where in the World Gem Hunt Quiz**: Voice-narrated map trivia game with streak counter and hints.
